# Tasks

Start only after the Owner's go-ahead on the issue, and after reading the Owner's answers to the points in design.md, "Open Questions". The tasks follow the planned answers. An answer marked "changes the spec" updates the requirement and these tasks first. Each proved file keeps everything its proofs call where it can, so that no contract loses its helpers across files (docs/verification.md, known issues), and ESLint limits a file to 400 lines, so unproved code goes in its own file when `shared/src/kinship.ts` reaches that. Invented people only, in code, fixtures, golden files and docs. Groups 1 to 3 make the rules, groups 4 to 6 the server side, group 7 the app, groups 8 and 9 wait for `add-tree-charts` (#126) and `add-interface-languages` (#113), and group 10 checks everything together.

## 2. Path rules (shared, proved)

- [ ] 2.1 Write `kinshipDegree` and `kinshipLine` in `shared/src/kinship.ts`, add the file to `shared/LemmaScript-files.txt`, and name `//@ contract kinship: Line and degree of kinship by birth` and `//@ contract kinship: Line and degree of kinship by marriage`. The ensures state: direct exactly when `up` or `down` is 0; the degree is `up + down`; kinship by marriage takes the degree of its part by birth; null exactly for `self`, `partner`, `stepSibling`, `coParentInLaw` and `foster` (scenarios "Great-grandfather", "Sibling", "Uncle", "First cousin and once removed", "Brother-in-law", "Daughter-in-law", "Wife"). Verify with `pnpm verify` and `pnpm verify:trace`, with vitest cases in `shared/test/kinship.test.ts` for each scenario, and with a deliberate bug (a sibling counted as degree 1) that makes `lsc check` fail.
- [ ] 2.2 Write `chainsUp` (index-based, like `reachFrom` in `relation.ts`) with `//@ contract kinship: Kinship is computed from Family links`. The ensures state: the start is at distance 0; within 12 generations every followed parent of a Person at distance d is at distance d + 1 at most; every distance is realised by a chain of followed links, its `order` falling at each step; only birth and adoptive links are followed (scenario "Godparent": the input holds Family links only, so no Role relates anyone). Verify with `pnpm verify` and `pnpm verify:trace`, with vitest cases for a ring-free pedigree with collapse and for the limit of 12, and with a deliberate bug (following `step` links) that makes `lsc check` fail.
- [ ] 2.3 Write `closestPath` with `//@ contract kinship: Paths in a fixed order`. The ensures state: its degree is at most the sum of the two distances to every common ancestor within the limits, and its chains meet only at its common ancestors. Add the lemma that a common ancestor with the smallest sum has shortest chains that meet only there to `shared/proofs/src/kinship.dfy` (scenario "Two cousinships"). Verify with `pnpm verify` and `pnpm verify:trace`, with vitest cases on a Person who is both second and third cousin, and with a deliberate bug (taking the largest sum) that makes `lsc check` fail.
- [ ] 2.4 Write `compareKinshipPaths` with `//@ contract kinship: Paths in a fixed order`, comparing ids by code units. The ensures state antisymmetry and 0 only for the same path, and the order of design.md, "Order" (scenarios "Two cousinships", "Brother-in-law and cousin", "Equal paths"). Add the transitivity lemma to `shared/proofs/src/kinship.dfy`. Verify with `pnpm verify` and `pnpm verify:trace`, with vitest cases for each scenario, and with a deliberate bug (kinship by marriage before kinship by birth at the same degree) that makes `lsc check` fail.
- [ ] 2.5 Write `isPossiblePath` with `//@ contract kinship: Kinship follows the evidence`. The ensures state: true exactly when a link of the path, the partner step of kinship by marriage included, has no Citation of normal or higher confidence; a confirming Citation on every weak link makes it false ("Weak father link", "Evidence improves"). It calls `isStrongLink` from `shared/src/relation.ts`, so mind the known issue "Cross-file contracts lose their helpers": give `isStrongLink` a contract over its parameter alone if that proves; otherwise pass each link's `strong` flag from the shell, which takes it from `isStrongLink`, and say so in the Scope entry of 2.9. Verify with `pnpm verify` and `pnpm verify:trace`, with vitest cases for both scenarios, and with a deliberate bug (`every` for `some`) that makes `lsc check` fail.
- [ ] 2.6 Write `isHalf` and `namesOwnParentOrChild` with `//@ contract kinship: Half, step, adoptive and foster kinship`. The ensures state: half exactly when the common ancestor is one Person reached through two different Families; no path by marriage or step names the reader's parent or child by birth or adoption through the same Family; foster and step links are never followed beyond their own pair ("Half-brother", "Stepmother", "Own child, not a stepchild", "Foster child of the grandparents"). Verify with `pnpm verify` and `pnpm verify:trace`, with vitest cases for each scenario and for one couple recorded as two Families (design.md, "Risks"), and with a deliberate bug (half for a full sibling) that makes `lsc check` fail.
- [ ] 2.7 Write `connectionChain` with `//@ contract kinship: Limits of the kinship search`. The ensures state: the chain is made of real Family links from one Person to the other, and is empty exactly when no chain exists ("Through two marriages", "No chain"). Verify with `pnpm verify` and `pnpm verify:trace`, with vitest cases for both scenarios and for the shortest chain, which is tested and not proved, and with a deliberate bug (a chain that skips a Person) that makes `lsc check` fail.
- [ ] 2.8 Write the unproved `kinshipPaths`: the steps of design.md, "Finding the paths", through the proved rules, with kinds, `steps`, `commonAncestors`, the limit of 100 paths and the work bound of 200,000 units, where the closest path is never cut and `more` is set when the bound is reached. Verify with vitest: "A wife who is also a cousin", "Double first cousins", "Full siblings", "Every Person on the way", "Many paths", "Records in another order" (the same links shuffled give the same answer), step-siblings and co-parents-in-law; a seeded test on invented trees of up to 30 Persons in which a naive search over every chain finds the same set of paths; and a test that asserts the units on a dense pedigree stay within the bound.
- [ ] 2.9 Docs, reference and explanation: add the Scope entries for these rules to `scripts/verification-trace/texts.mjs` (what each proves and what the shell and tests cover), add `shared/src/kinship.ts` to `SWAP_CHECK` after reading the generated `.dfy.gen` for the swapped comparison, and regenerate `docs/verification.md` with `node scripts/verification-trace.mjs`.
