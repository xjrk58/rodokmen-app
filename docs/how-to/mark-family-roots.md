# How to mark Family roots and link Members to their Persons

This guide shows the Owner how to tell Rodokmen where the family starts, and who each Member is in the Tree. Only the Owner can do either. Why the Family roots matter is in [The relation to the family](../explanation/relation-to-the-family.md).

## Mark a Family root

Until there is a Family root, everyone in the Tree is shown as family, and Settings asks you to mark one.

1. Open **Settings**. Under **Family roots**, click **Mark a Family root**.
2. Type the name of the Person the family descends from, and pick them from the list.

The Person is listed with the **Family root** mark, and every Person's relation to the family is worked out again at once. To mark a couple, mark both partners.

You can also mark a Person from their page: click **Mark as a Family root**.

To unmark one, click **Remove** next to them in Settings, or **No longer a Family root** on their page.

Marking and unmarking are logged like accepted Proposals: they appear in the Person's **History**, where you can undo them.

## Link a Member to their Person

1. Open **Settings**. Under **Family members**, then **Members**, each Member shows **In the Tree:** and their Person, or "not linked".
2. Click **Link to a person** next to the Member, type their name, and pick their Person.

That Member's tree now opens on their own Person unless their device remembers another Focus Person, and the **Me** button in the tree returns there.

To change the link, click **Change** and pick another Person. To remove it, click **Remove the link**.

If the Member is not in the Tree yet, add them first with a Proposal (such as **Baptism or birth**), accept it, and then link them.
