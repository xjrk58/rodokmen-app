# How to read a scan and check it

This guide shows you, as a Member, how to have a register page read by the server and turn the reading into a Proposal: add a photo of the page, check each entry against the scan, choose who each person is, and submit. It works the same on a phone and on a computer. It needs scan reading switched on by the Owner ([switch scan reading on](switch-on-scan-reading.md)); without it, **Read a scan** is not offered, and the forms by hand still are.

The page you add is sent to Anthropic's API to be read ([what is sent, and why](../explanation/scan-reading.md)). Nothing reaches the Tree until you submit and an Editor accepts.

## Add the page

1. Open **Review**, then **Propose**, and choose **Read a scan**. It says how many readings you have left today.
2. Choose **Add a page** and take a photo of the page, or pick a saved image. Add up to five pages, in order. To read one entry only, drag over it on the image; **Whole page** clears the mark. Only the part you marked is sent.
3. Under **What you know about the page**, fill in what you know; all of it is optional, and all of it helps:
   - the register: find it in the Tree, or type it under **Register (if not in the Tree)**;
   - **Page**, the **Place** the entries are from, and **From year** and **To year**;
   - **Link to the scan online**, if you found the page on an archive's portal. The link is kept for the Citation; the page itself is read only from the image you added;
   - **Note for the Editor**, for anything else, such as "baptisms, left page". It goes with the page to help the reading, and into your Proposal's note.
4. Choose **Read**.

A link to an archive viewer is not an image: the server never opens it. Save the page from the viewer as an image, or photograph the screen, and add that.

The reading takes a minute or two. It is saved as a Draft on your device, so you can leave and come back to it from **Review → Drafts**. If you lose the connection, the reading goes on and the screen asks again when you are back online.

## Check the reading

The screen shows the scan and, under it on a phone or beside it on a wider screen, the entries as they were read. Values marked **read by AI** are still as the machine read them.

For each entry:

1. Compare the date, the house number (čp.) and every person's names with the scan, and correct what is wrong. A value the reader could not read is left empty and says why, such as "House number: could not be read (faded)"; fill it in only if you can read it.
2. Choose who each person is. Under each one are the Persons of the Tree whose name fits, best first, with the reasons: **name**, **House**, **years fit**, **Place**, **family**. Tap the right one, or **New Person** if nobody fits. Nobody is created unless you choose so.
3. Choose **Confirm entry**. Its **read by AI** marks go away; a value you correct loses its mark at once.

An entry you cannot check, or that is not about the family, can stay out: tick **Leave this entry out**. **The page as read**, under each scan, shows the whole transcription.

## Keep the next steps

Under **Next steps** the reading suggests Research leads, such as finding the parents' marriage some years before a baptism, with the register and the archive to look in. Choose **Keep as a lead** for each one worth following up; it becomes a Research lead every Member sees, and **Kept as a Research lead: open it** takes you there. **Dismiss** the rest. Nothing about the family is stated by a suggestion ([work with Research leads](work-with-research-leads.md)).

## Submit it

1. Under **Evidence**, check the register and the page. The reading makes one Citation per page, with its image and its transcription. Its **Confidence** is at most normal: a machine's reading is never "certain".
2. Write a **Note for the Editor** if there is anything to say.
3. Choose **Submit for review**, or **Save Draft** to finish later.

The Proposal waits in review like any other. The Editor sees which values are still **read by AI**, and accepts or rejects the Proposal as a whole.

## When a reading fails

The screen says why, such as "The reading was cut off: the scans hold too much for one reading. Read fewer pages, or mark a crop." Choose **Fill in the form by hand** to enter the entry yourself, or start a new reading with fewer pages or a marked entry. A failed reading counts against your readings of the day. When none are left, the forms by hand are still there, and reading works again the next day.
