# See how you are related

Find out how any Person in the Tree is related to you: the term, the line and degree, and every path between you. Why it works this way: [Kinship](../explanation/kinship.md). The terms: [Kinship terms and degrees](../reference/kinship-terms.md).

Kinship needs your own Person: the Person who is you in the Tree, linked to your account by the Owner. If your account is not linked yet, start with [ask to be linked](#ask-to-be-linked-to-your-own-person).

## Read how a Person is related to you

1. Open the Person's page, from search, the People list or the tree.
2. Under the name, the section **How you are related** ("Jak jste příbuzní") gives the term ("praděd", "second cousin"), the line and degree ("direct line, 3rd degree"), and the common ancestors, each a link to their page.
3. If the term reads **possibly** ("možná") with a "?", a link on the way has no Citation of normal or higher confidence. Open the path to see which link is weak, and add the evidence in a Proposal to take the mark away.

In search results and the People list, each Person's term follows the life years. In the tree, the strip above the boxes gives the Focus Person's kinship to you; tap it to open their page at the section. Your own Person reads "you" ("vy").

## List every path

1. On the Person's page, in **How you are related**, press **and 2 more paths** (the number varies).
2. Each path shows its term, line and degree, its marks (possibly, half, through adoption), and every Person on the way with the step to the next ("otec: …", "syn: …"). Each Person is a link to their page.
3. If the list ends with **there may be more**, the search stopped at its limit of 100 paths or of work; the paths shown are the closest.

A Person with no kinship to you shows how you are connected instead ("connected through 2 marriages", with the chain), or that the Tree records no kinship between you.

## Ask to be linked to your own Person

1. Find yourself in the Tree with search, and open your page.
2. In **How you are related**, press **This is me** ("Toto jsem já").
3. Settings shows the request as waiting for the Owner. To withdraw it, press **Withdraw** there or on the page.

The Owner confirms or refuses the request in Settings ([how the Owner links Members](mark-family-roots.md#confirm-or-refuse-a-request-to-be-linked)). Once it is confirmed, kinship appears everywhere in the app. If the Owner refuses it, the page offers **This is me** again.

The Owner links their own account in Settings directly.
