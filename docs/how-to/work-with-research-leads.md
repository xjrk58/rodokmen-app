# How to work with Research leads

This guide shows you, as a Member, how to write a Research lead, take it on, record what you searched, close it, and turn what you found into a Proposal. It also shows how to accept or dismiss a lead the gap finder suggests. Why leads change at once, without review: [Research leads](../explanation/research-leads.md). Their fields and statuses: [Research leads reference](../reference/research-leads.md).

## Write a lead

Start where the question comes up:

- **On a person page**, under **Research leads**, choose **Write a lead**. The Person is filled in.
- **On the map**, tap a family pin. Its sheet lists the open leads of the Place; choose **Write a lead here**. The Place is filled in.
- **In the Leads tab**, choose **Write a lead**.

Then:

1. Write the **Question** ("Find Jan's baptism") and **Why** (what is known so far, and why you think it is there).
2. Under **Register, Persons and Places**, add what you know: the **Archive**, the register Source, the years (**From year**, **To year**), the **Pages**, and more Persons and Places.
3. Choose **Write the lead**.

The lead is saved at once under your name, and every Member sees it.

## Find leads

- **The Leads tab** lists every lead, open and in progress first. Filter by **Status**, by Place, by **Archive**, and by **Who is working on it** (**Anyone** or **Me**).
- **A person page** lists the leads about that Person.
- **A family pin's sheet** on the map lists the open leads of that Place; **All leads here** opens them in the Leads tab.

## Take a lead on

Open the lead and choose **I am working on it**. The lead shows as in progress, with your name. To change anything else, choose **Edit**, change the fields, and choose **Save**.

If someone changed the lead while you had it open, saving is refused: reload the lead and make your change again.

## Record a search

Record every search, also one that found nothing.

1. Open the lead. Under **Record a search**, keep **Searched** with the lead's register ticked, or untick it and name the **Archive**.
2. Fill in **From year**, **To year** and **Pages**.
3. Tick **Found what the lead asks** if you found it.
4. Under **What was found, or what was there instead**, write what you saw.
5. Choose **Record the search**.

The search appears under **Searches**, with who looked and when.

## Close a lead

- **Nothing found:** fill in the search as above and choose **Record and close as not found**. A lead can close as not found only with a search, so this records the search and closes the lead in one step.
- **Found, with a fact for the Tree:** see the next section. The lead is marked found when an Editor accepts your Proposal.
- **Found, with nothing for the Tree** (for example, the answer is that the register is lost): write the **Outcome** and choose **Close as found**.

To open a closed lead again, choose **Reopen**.

## Start a Proposal from a lead

1. Open the lead. Under **Start from this lead**, choose the kind of finding: **Baptism or birth**, **Marriage**, **Death or burial**, **A new Name**, **A correction**, or **Notes and Labels**.
2. The form starts with the lead's Persons, Place and register. Complete it with the Citation, as for any Proposal, and submit it.

The Proposal names the lead. When an Editor accepts it, the lead is marked found and links to the Proposal. If the Proposal is rejected, the lead stays as it was; if it is undone later, the lead opens again.

## Accept or dismiss a suggested lead

The gap finder suggests leads for gaps in the Tree, such as a Relative with no baptism Citation. They are listed apart, under **Suggested leads** in the Leads tab and on the person page of the Person they are about, each with the register or archive to check and the years.

- **Make it a lead** turns the suggestion into a lead you can work on.
- **Dismiss** removes it; it is not suggested again.

Only Members decide on suggested leads; Agents do not.
